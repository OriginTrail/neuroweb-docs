# Bridges

TRAC, the utility token of the OriginTrail Decentralized Knowledge Graph, can be moved between Ethereum and NeuroWeb using [**Snowbridge**](https://app.snowbridge.network/) — **a trustless, Polkadot-native bridge secured entirely by the consensus mechanisms of Ethereum and Polkadot**, rather than third-party services or multisigs.

### Bridging TRAC between Ethereum and NeuroWeb

Bridging works in both directions (Ethereum → NeuroWeb and NeuroWeb → Ethereum) via the [Snowbridge UI](https://app.snowbridge.network/). For detailed step-by-step instructions, see the [Ethereum-NeuroWeb TRAC Bridge](../ethereum-neuroweb-trac-bridge.md) page.

{% hint style="info" %}
To use your bridged TRAC for **staking** or **publishing on the DKG**, you must first map your NeuroWeb (Substrate) address to your EVM address using the [mapping interface](https://neuroweb.ai/evm).
{% endhint %}
